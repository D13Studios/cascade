Need help? Email d13studios.cascade@gmail.com. The quickest way is in the game: open Settings → SUPPORT, which starts an email with your player ID already filled in.

Common questions

I reinstalled the game. Where's my progress?
On iPhone, your account is kept in your Keychain, so it comes back on its own when you reinstall, and on a new iPhone if iCloud Keychain is on. If it doesn't, open Settings → ACCOUNT & RECOVERY CODE on your old device to see your recovery code, then use USE A CODE on the new one.

My purchase didn't arrive.
Restart the game while online; interrupted purchases are delivered at launch. For things you buy once (like the Starter Pack, Remove Ads or the Lifetime Card), use Restore Purchases in the Store. If it's still missing, email us with your player ID.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Someone is being abusive in chat.
Tap their message to report or block them. Players reported by several different people are muted automatically.

How do I turn off personalised ads or delete my data?
Settings → PRIVACY. You can switch personalised ads and analytics off, or delete your account and its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
