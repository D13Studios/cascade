Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the CASCADE mobile game ("the game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. CASCADE is made by Division 13 Studios ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions, email d13studios.cascade@gmail.com.

The short version. We don't ask for your real name, email or phone number. The game creates an anonymous player account so your progress, guild and purchases are safe. With your permission, ad partners can show ads that suit you and we collect anonymous gameplay data to improve the game. You can change that choice, or delete your account and its data, at any time in Settings.

Reminder notifications (for example "your energy is full") are scheduled on your phone. They only appear if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off in your phone's Settings.

2. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We use these service providers to run the game, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: player accounts, cloud save, leaderboards, server code, analytics, crash reporting).
  • Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with, to show ads. If you allow it, they may use your advertising identifier to personalise ads.
  • Apple, for in-app purchases, notifications and iCloud Keychain.

Your chosen player name, guild membership, chat messages and leaderboard scores are visible to other players.

3. Your choices

  • Ads and analytics: Settings → PRIVACY lets you turn personalised ads and analytics on or off at any time. You can also control tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Chat: you can report or block players, and you don't have to use chat or guilds at all.
  • Delete your account: Settings → PRIVACY → DELETE MY ACCOUNT permanently deletes your online account, name, guild membership, cloud save and league scores, and asks Unity to delete your analytics data. Chat messages you already sent stay visible under your old name until they roll off the chat history, which only keeps the most recent messages.
  • Access or correct your data: email us with the player ID shown when you tap SUPPORT in Settings.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account. Chat keeps only the most recent messages in each channel; older ones are deleted. Analytics data is kept for up to 2 years. When you delete your account, we delete your account data promptly, and our providers remove any remaining copies under their own retention schedules.

5. Children

CASCADE is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game's online features, contact us and we will delete their data.

6. Your rights (UK, EU and elsewhere)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email d13studios.cascade@gmail.com. Where we rely on consent (personalised ads, analytics), we only collect that data if you accept. Otherwise we rely on what's needed to provide the game you asked for. If you're in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising unless you accept personalised ads, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings → PRIVACY.

7. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by our service providers, who may process it outside the UK (for example in the United States) under appropriate safeguards.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the game.
